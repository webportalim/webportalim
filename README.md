## Featured projects

### MotionSmith Pro V2 — AI Animation Inside Sequencer

[![Watch MotionSmith Pro V2 — AI Animation Inside Sequencer](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/webportalim/MotionSmith-AI-Pro-Text-to-Motion-for-Sequencer/main/assets/motionsmith-pro-v2-cover.png)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ptCTMbEFt-Q)

**Now available on Fab.** Direct character motion inside **Unreal Engine 5.8 Sequencer** with text prompts, route-aware Gameplay Motion, segment continuation, and Control Rig refinement.

Describe the action, shape the route, build the motion segment, and polish the performance in the same sequence as your cameras and shot timing.

[**Get Pro V2 on Fab**](https://www.fab.com/listings/3d83439b-d1b1-4345-865e-08ed69b995b2) · [Watch the video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ptCTMbEFt-Q) · [Product overview](https://github.com/webportalim/MotionSmith-AI-Pro-Text-to-Motion-for-Sequencer) · [Documentation](https://motionsmithai.blogspot.com/p/motionsmith-docs-v2.html)

### MotionSmith Surface — AI Texturing for Blender

Generate textures and create **PBR materials inside Blender** with a local ComfyUI backend. Work with image and text guidance, multi-view texturing, UV baking, and PBR maps.

[**Repository & installation**](https://github.com/webportalim/MotionSmith-Surface) · [Documentation](https://motionsmithai.blogspot.com/p/motionsmith-surface-docs.html) · [Product page](https://motionsmithai.blogspot.com/p/motionsmith-surface.html)

### MotionSmith V1 — AI Animation Studio for Unreal Engine

The original MotionSmith animation workflow for **Unreal Engine 5.8**: text-to-motion generation, timeline segments, pose editing, supported-character retargeting, and AnimationSequence export.

[**Repository**](https://github.com/webportalim/MotionSmith-Kimodo-Animation-Studio) · [Fab listing](https://www.fab.com/listings/21c531e5-02b0-4d4a-960d-8cceab5dba34) · [V1 documentation](https://motionsmithai.blogspot.com/p/motionsmith-v1-docs.html)
